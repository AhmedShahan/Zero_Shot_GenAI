🤖 "The Ballad of the Techie Brain" 🤖

In a lab where coffee reigns supreme,
Gen AI woke up from a data dream.
It painted cats with dragon wings,
And wrote love songs for vending machines.

Machine Learning joined with flair,
"Let’s find some patterns, if you dare!"
It trained on spam and stocks and cheese,
Predicting moods and bakery fees.

Computer Vision blinked awake,
“Is that a dog... or chocolate cake?”
It scanned a face, it scanned a chair—
It tagged my mom as a grizzly bear! 😅

Neural Nets with tangled threads,
Whispered math inside their heads.
"Layer by layer, truth shall bloom!
After we fix that loss, ka-boom!"

Backpropagation did its dance,
Tweaking weights like techno trance.
It failed, it tried, then caught its stride,
Till the cat and dragon finally aligned.

Gen AI giggled, “Look what I made—
A poem, a meme, and a marmalade!”
Computer Vision blinked, confused:
“Why’s the marmalade... wearing shoes?”

So if you hear code laugh and snore,
Don’t worry, it’s just models galore—
Learning life one byte at a time,
In a world where data learns to rhyme. 🧠✨
